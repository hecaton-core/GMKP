# GMKP - "Good Morning Kiss Provider"

## GENERAL CONCEPT :
"GMKP" is a minimalistic Web-App designed to provide a daily dose of affection to a 
desperate girlfriend.
In the perfect application of the pavlovian principle, the girlfriend will press the
button, receive a fix of fresh affection, and continue pressing the button until her
needs are fully satisfied.
In the meantime the boyfriend can work on his shit or play videogames peacefully.
To summarize, this was made as a modest contribution to world peace.

## HOW TO USE :
1- Open the application
2- Press the kiss button
3- Repeat until emotionally satisfied
4- For advanced users : reach 1000 kisses
5- Marry the dev' (optional, but recommended)

## HOW I GOT THIS IDEA :
My girlfriend challenged my creativity, asking me to demonstrate it on the next morning...
So I started coding this during the evening, because that's what a man does when his girl ask
for something I guess !


## CURRENT FEATURES :
* A large selection of 20 different and personalized affectionate messages available
* A special gift will be delivered after every full-cycle, maintaining the girlfriend's interest
* If 1000 clicks are reached, the girlfriend will be considered wife-material and will consequently 
be treated to a special and unique gift.
